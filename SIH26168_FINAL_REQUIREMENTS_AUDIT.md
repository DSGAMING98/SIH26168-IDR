Dataset eligibility: IO-VNBD S1 and Google Smartphone Decimeter 2022 have evaluable inertial/GNSS/reference windows. WHU local files lack the required IMU stream; MoRPI lacks the required GNSS/reference/magnetometer combination; PPC and GREAT have no usable local payload. Inventory is not validation. See the [Phase 8 dataset report](technical-evidence/docs/phase8_cross_dataset_validation.md).

**Verdict: NOT READY for full final-solution compliance; ready to demonstrate as a research prototype.** The official brief explicitly calls for under-10% drift, lane-level positioning, runtime road matching and an external-IMU edge pathway. Those are not demonstrated. A judge should see the working causal system, transparent OOD/recovery design and measured failure modes, with the accuracy limit stated plainly.
